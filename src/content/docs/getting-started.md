## Install

Download the Windows installer from the [download page](/en/download) and run it. No account is required.

## Transcribe a file

1. Open video2text and pick a local audio or video file.
2. Choose your language (or let it auto-detect).
3. Start transcription. Everything runs on your machine.

## Next steps

- Enable GPU acceleration for faster transcription.
- Connect a local model for summaries, or bring your own online model key.
