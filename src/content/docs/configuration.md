## Models

video2text ships with a built-in offline model. You can also plug in an online
transcription endpoint using your own key (BYOK).

## GPU

GPU acceleration uses CUDA when a supported dedicated GPU is present. Otherwise
the app falls back to CPU automatically.

## Online models (BYOK)

Online models are bring-your-own-key. Your keys are stored locally on your
machine and never shared with us.
