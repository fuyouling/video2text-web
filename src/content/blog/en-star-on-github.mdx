If video2text has been helpful to you — whether you use it to transcribe
lectures, meetings, or your favorite videos — we'd love your support.

The easiest way to help is to **give the project a star on GitHub**:

[⭐ Star video2text on GitHub](https://github.com/fuyouling/video2text)

Stars help more people discover the tool, and they keep us motivated to keep
improving it. If you have feedback or run into any issues, feel free to open an
issue on the repository. Thank you for being part of the community!
