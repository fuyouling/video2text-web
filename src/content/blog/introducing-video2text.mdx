We built video2text to turn audio and video into accurate text and smart
summaries fast — right on your own machine.

video2text transcribes audio and video with faster-whisper, and can summarize
with a local model or your own online model (BYOK). Export clean text, JSON, and
subtitles — no subscription required.

The desktop app is free. A one-time Pro upgrade adds batch, incremental
processing and more. Online models are bring-your-own-key.
