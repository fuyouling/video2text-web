We built video2text because transcription should not require uploading your
private recordings to someone else's servers.

video2text runs entirely on your machine. It transcribes audio and video with
faster-whisper, summarizes with a local model, and exports clean text, JSON, and
subtitles — no cloud, no subscription.

The desktop app is free. A one-time Pro upgrade adds batch, incremental
processing and more. Online models are bring-your-own-key.
